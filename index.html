How it works

Up and running in minutes

No developers needed. No complex setup. Just sign up and go.

1
Create your business account
Register with your email, add your business name, upload your logo, choose your brand colour, and write a welcome message. Takes under two minutes.
2
Build your FAQ knowledge base
Add the questions your customers ask most often, along with perfect answers. The widget uses these to reply automatically — no AI hallucinations, just your own words.
3
Copy your installation code
Grab your unique <script> tag from the dashboard and paste it before the </body> tag of your website. The widget appears instantly.
4
Watch leads and chats roll in
Your dashboard updates in real time. See every conversation, collect leads automatically when the bot can't answer, and export your contact list any time.
